# 🧭 City Soul Navigator

A smart navigation concept that goes beyond the fastest route. City Soul Navigator recommends routes based on **user preferences, mood, accessibility needs, and points of interest**, so every trip through a city feels personal.

**Currently integrated cities:** London and Chennai

---

## ✨ Features

- **Preference-based routing**: routes tailored to what you enjoy
- **Mood-aware suggestions**: recommendations that match how you feel
- **Accessibility-friendly navigation**: routes that consider accessibility needs
- **Points of interest**: discover interesting places along your route
- **Nearby places discovery**: find places around you
- **Save memorable locations**: bookmark the places you love
- **Share local experiences**: share tips and stories about the city
- **Multi-city support**: London and Chennai, with more cities planned

---

## 🗺️ How It Works

1. Choose a city (London or Chennai).
2. Enter your start point and destination.
3. Select your preferences, mood, and any accessibility needs.
4. Get a recommended route with points of interest along the way.
5. Save places you like and share your experiences.
